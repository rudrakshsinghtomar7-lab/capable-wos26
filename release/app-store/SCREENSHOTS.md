## Current Apple image requirements

Checked 15 November 2026: Apple's required iPhone group is “iPhone with Dynamic Island (medium display)”. Its listed portrait sizes are 1179 × 2556 or 1206 × 2622. Export actual screenshots at an accepted size; preserve the device's layout rather than stretching a different layout. PNG/JPEG/JPG are accepted and images must not contain an alpha channel.

If iPad remains enabled, also prepare the required 13-inch iPad set after verifying the iPad layout. Listed portrait sizes are 2064 × 2752 or 2048 × 2732. Check the current App Store Connect upload slot before exporting.

At least one screenshot is required for each required device size; Apple permits up to ten. A preview video is optional.

Use the real shipped UI. Captions may explain functionality but must not advertise government endorsement, live jobs, automatic payslip extraction, claim submission, redeemable offers, guaranteed eligibility or certified accessibility.

Source: [Apple screenshot specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications/).
