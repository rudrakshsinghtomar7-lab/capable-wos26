Capable privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026 · Applies to the Capable iPhone app and the Capable web app

Draft. This policy is not final yet. The publisher's name and contact details will be added before Capable is released on the App Store.

The short version

  • Your answers stay on your device. Capable has no server and no account.
  • Capable does not collect, sell or share your information, and has no ads or tracking.
  • Your answers are only kept after you close Capable if you turn on Save my answers.
  • You can delete everything at any time: Privacy and your data, then Delete everything.

Who we are

Capable is published by [publisher name] in Queensland, Australia. In this policy, "we" means the publisher. Capable is not connected to Centrelink, Services Australia, the NDIA or the Queensland Government.

What Capable stores, and where

Everything is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. We cannot see it.

  • Your answers, only if you turn on saving: your wages, your answers to the pension questions, your concession answers, your job needs and hours, and an NDIS balance if you enter one. If saving is off, they are forgotten when you close Capable.
  • Settings with no personal details: whether Easy Read is on, whether you have seen the welcome screen, whether you last used the demo, and whether you closed a tip.

The demo uses a made-up person. Nothing you change in the demo is saved.

Some answers can say something about your health or disability, so Capable keeps them on your device rather than sending them anywhere.

What leaves your device

Capable does not send your answers anywhere. A few features involve something outside Capable, and only when you use them:

  • Voice input (Ask Capable). Only when you press Speak. In the iPhone app, speech is recognised on your iPhone only; if your iPhone cannot do that, voice input stays off. In a web browser, the browser does the recognition and may send your voice to its provider, such as Apple or Google. Capable never receives the audio.
  • Read aloud. Uses the voices on your device. Some web browsers use online voices.
  • Payslip photo. The photo is shown on screen so you can read the amount. It stays on your device and Capable does not save it.
  • Share. Sends the summary text only to the app or person you choose.
  • Links to government websites open those websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Permissions on iPhone

  • Microphone and speech recognition: only for voice input, when you press Speak.
  • Camera: only if you choose to photograph a payslip.

You can turn these off at any time in the iPhone Settings app. Everything else in Capable still works.

Information others may collect

  • Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics, but only if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your iPhone settings. These do not include your answers.
  • GitHub, which hosts the web app, may log technical information such as your IP address when you open it. See the GitHub privacy statement.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools of our own.

Deleting your information

Open Privacy and your data in Capable and choose Delete everything. Deleting the iPhone app also deletes everything it stored. The web app and the iPhone app keep separate storage, so delete in each one you use.

Children

Capable is designed for adults. It does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Capable ever starts collecting or sending information, we will update this policy and say so in the app before it happens.
