Welcome to Capable

Work, money, and what you’re owed — in one place, for people with disability in Queensland.

    Everything here runs on a fortnight, not a month — because that is how your pay and your pension actually arrive.

    Help and who to call

    If a number here disagrees with a letter from Centrelink

    Believe the letter, not this app. Everything in Capable is an estimate worked out from the published rates. It does not know about your full circumstances, and it is not connected to Centrelink or the NDIA. Nothing you do here changes your payment or reports your income.

    If the difference matters to you, ring Services Australia and ask them to explain the figure on the letter. You will not get in trouble for asking.

    Services Australia — Disability, Sickness and Carers line

    13 27 17
    Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. For questions about your Disability Support Pension, reporting income, and your concession card.

    National Disability Insurance Agency

    1800 800 110
    Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm. For questions about your NDIS plan, your budgets, and what your funding can pay for.

    If you would rather not use the phone

    The National Relay Service passes calls on for people who are deaf, hard of hearing, or have a speech disability. Details are at accesshub.gov.au (National Relay Service, opens in a new tab). You can also use your myGov account to message Services Australia in writing.

    Reporting your income

    Capable does not report anything for you. You still need to report your fortnightly income to Services Australia the way you normally do.

    About this app

    Capable is a demonstration. All figures shown are estimates for one made-up demo user in Queensland. Nothing here is financial advice, and no data is sent anywhere — everything runs in your browser and is forgotten when you close the tab.

    Payment rules used: Disability Support Pension income free area of $226.00 per fortnight, then 50 cents less payment for every dollar earned above it (single, as at 1 July 2026). Check your own situation with Services Australia, and your plan with the NDIA.

    Built for WOS26. Queensland only.

    Accessibility

    Capable targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Accessibility is the product here, not a feature bolted on at the end.

    What we built for. Every control is real HTML that works with a keyboard, a switch, or a screen reader. Zoom is never disabled and the app follows your phone’s own text size setting. Nothing is signalled by colour alone. Dragging is never the only way to do something. Nothing flashes, autoplays, or times out.

    No account, on purpose. There is no login, so there is no password or puzzle standing between you and your own money information — which is how we meet SC 3.3.8 Accessible Authentication. Nothing you type leaves this device, and there is no server to leak it.

    What we tested with. Keyboard only; the largest iOS text size; 400% zoom down to a 320px wide window; light and dark; and with the app offline. Figures and contrast ratios were checked by script rather than by eye.

    What we know is imperfect. This has not yet been tested by disabled people, which is the test that actually counts. Easy Read and read-aloud are not built yet. The demo figures are made up, and the pension rate used here is an estimate rather than a verified current figure.

    If something here blocked you, that is a bug and we want to know.

    Your saved answers

    Capable remembers the hours, needs and wages you enter, so putting your phone down does not cost you the form. It is kept on this device only.
