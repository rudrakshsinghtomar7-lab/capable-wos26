Help and who to call

If a number here disagrees with a letter from Centrelink

Believe the letter, not this app. Capable gives estimates. It does not know your full circumstances and is not connected to Centrelink or the NDIA. Nothing you do here changes your payment or reports your income.

If you would rather not use the phone

The National Relay Service can help people who are deaf, hard of hearing or have a speech disability: accesshub.gov.au (National Relay Service, opens outside Capable).

Reporting your income

Capable does not report anything for you. Keep reporting your income to Services Australia the way you normally do.

Accessibility

Capable is targeting WCAG 2.2 Level AA. It has not had a formal accessibility assessment, and has not yet been tested by disabled people — the test that counts.

Every control works with a keyboard, switch or screen reader. Zoom is never disabled and text follows your device’s text size. Nothing is shown by colour alone, dragging is never the only way to do something, and nothing times out. Voice is always optional: every voice action has a button.

If something here blocked you, that is a bug.

Working offline

Once opened, everything in Capable works offline, including the calculations and Easy Read. Links to official websites, and voice input in some web browsers, need a connection.
